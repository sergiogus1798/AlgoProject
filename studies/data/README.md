# studies/data — are the data fit to judge with?

Studies of the inputs rather than of a strategy: `feedQuality/` (encargo 17) and `spread/`.

| folder | what it answers |
|---|---|
| `feedQuality/` | Is each M1 feed sound, and from which year (step 4, the preflight's warning); how much of a strategy's profit sits on anomalous minutes (step 8, after the gate). Built 2026-09-26 on the owner's answers to the 16 decisions |
| `spread/` | What Darwinex's ticks say the spread really is, whether it stays constant against price, what SQX should carry per segment (step 4); and each strategy repriced at that spread instead of SQX's flat one — the cheap stand-in for a DATATICK retest (step 8). Built 2026-09-27, XAUUSD and USDJPY first |
