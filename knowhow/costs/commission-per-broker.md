# `commission.use` is the most restrictive CONFIRMED broker, never one account's own figure
Owner, 2026-09-29: every `costs.commission` carries `brokers: {<name>: {method, value, unit, source, date, confirmed, note?}}`. `confirmed: true` only off the firm's own page, dated; everything else is `value: null` with a `note`. `core.commission.most_restrictive` prices every confirmed broker in dollars at each segment's median price (`build`/`oos1`/`oos2`, cached by `studies.data.spread.onboard` in `summary.json`'s `proposal`) and picks whichever charges most where they disagree MOST — not the cheapest segment, not an average. `use_value()` returns the winner in the asset's own unit.
