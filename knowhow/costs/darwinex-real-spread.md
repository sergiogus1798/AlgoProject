# Darwinex's real spread is 3–8× what `assets/` declares for XAUUSD, and not constant even relative to price. No units bug on the JPY pairs, and the Ficha's real curve beating SQX's is by design
- ⚠️ **Correction:** this card said "USDJPY 4.7 → 10.4 points mean" since it was written (2026-09-27) — never what the code computed. See Evidence: no factor-10 or points/pips bug anywhere in the pipeline.
- XAUUSD mean opening spread 9 → 63 points (2017 → 2026), 0.69–1.89 bp of price (2020 = 1.96× the median year). Declared: 5 / 10 points.
- The first tick of a full hour costs ~13 % more than an average minute; hour 00 (rollover) 2.1× the day on gold. Price a trade at its own minute, never at the day's mean.
- On bid bars SQX adds the whole spread to the ask side: a long pays the spread standing at entry, a short the one at exit. Reprice with that minute's spread (`studies/data/spread/reprice.py`).
- Indices' daily spread barely follows price (power-law exponent DJ30 −0.01, USA500 −0.10, USATEC −0.14, Nikkei −0.63; DAX +0.56): it moves by regime. SQX's MC Retest draws ONE spread per simulation (`RandomizeSpread.java`, uniform, 0.1-point steps) → band the DAILY mean, not the minute (`studies.data.spread.bands`); the owner pinned `relativo` for the indices (2026-09-27).
- Before Oct 2017 there are no ticks: modelled from Dukascopy volatility (gold, 19 % error backwards) or volatility + price (USDJPY, 10 %). Unverified against anything pre-2017.
- The Ficha's "spread y slippage reales" curve can legitimately beat SQX's own curve (owner decision, no code fix): see Evidence for why and the one-line "?" that now explains it in `fichacurves.py`.

## Evidence
- **The "4.7 → 10.4" correction:** `studies.data.spread.scan`'s own `summary.json` of 2026-09-27 already had USDJPY "puntos equivalentes" 0.48 (build) / 0.57 (oos1) / 1.17 (oos2); a fresh recomputation from the same Darwinex ticks on 2026-09-30 gives yearly mean points 0.4 (2017) → 1.0 (2026) — matching `assets/symbols/USDJPY.yaml`'s `spread_is/oos/oos2` (0.49/0.59/1.16, × 1.25) almost exactly. AUDJPY the same way: yearly mean 0.7–1.6, matching 1.61/1.27/1.92.
- **No units bug:** `assets/`'s "points" = `instrument.tick_size` (0.01 for both pairs) = SQX's own `INSTRUMENTS.TICKSIZE` (`SQX_w2/user/data/data.db`: `USDJPY_ftmo` `TICKSIZE 0.01 DEFAULTSPREAD 0.9`; `AUDJPY_ftmo` `TICKSIZE 0.01 DEFAULTSPREAD 1.4`). MT5's own "point" (often 0.001, a pipette on a 3-decimal JPY quote) is a different tool's unit and never enters this pipeline. The one points→price conversion is in `studies/data/spread/inputs._task_costs()` (`float(node.get("spread")) * tick`), applied once, reading the project's OWN `<Chart spread=…>`.
- **Why the real curve can beat SQX's:** the Ficha reprices at the RAW measured spread (`reprice.paid()`, no safety factor); SQX's curve is whatever spread the project's OWN `<Setup>` charged at build time. Two `Test_USDJPY_donchianUpperCrossUp_*` harvests, repriced 2026-09-30: `..._H1` (Results, 2026-09-30) SQX charged 0.65 pt spread / 0.325 pt slippage on IS and OOS vs real mean 0.365 pt → real+slippage **+20.0 %** over SQX (SQX ran pessimistic, the owner's × 1.25 overkill). `..._M30` (Results, 2026-09-29) SQX still charges a leftover 0.1 pt spread / 0.05 pt slippage — the pre-2026-09-27 the5ers factory default, never rebuilt — vs the same ~0.39 pt real mean → real+slippage **−21.8 %** (SQX ran optimistic here). Both are the pipeline working as designed: a stored harvest carries whatever its own project charged, not what `assets/` declares today (`studies/CLAUDE.md`: "a stored result covers whatever window it was last retested over"). Commission and swap are untouched by `reprice.adjust`/`slip` in both.
- `python3 -m studies.data.spread.scan` → `AlgoData/spread/<tick feed>/summary.json`, `spread.html` (2026-09-27).
- Repricing harvests (`studies.data.spread.report`): XAU_ISOOS_ejemplo OOS 66 → 63 strategies net > 0, net −16 % median; USDJPY_emaCross_H1 OOS 40 → 37, −34 %; Test_USDJPY_donchianUpperCrossUp_M30 OOS 191 → 186, −19 %.
