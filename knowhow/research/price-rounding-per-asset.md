# SQX fills sit on the tick grid, split over the two ticks around the cost: the card says which way the repricer goes
- Rule: card field `price_rounding: tick | none` (`core.assetwrite.set_price_rounding`), measured by `engines.market.rounding.measure(symbol, trades)` on any trades export; `reprice.costs` refuses a card without it, but step 8 (`gate.inputs.ensure_rounding`, owner R1 = A, 2026-10-16) measures a missing one on the build's trades (first 300 strategies) and writes it; a tie writes `tick`. Family/structural markets (steps 10/18.5) are not covered: they still refuse until measured by hand. Measured 2026-10-15 over 29 batches of `strategyPermutations`: **`none`** XAUUSD, USA500, DAX40, AUS200 (retest at oos1: tick 20,5 % vs none 2,4 %); **`tick`** DJ30, UK100, HK50 (127 NIKKEI225/DAX40/USATEC H1 mothers, retest at oos1, both under 1 %: indistinguishable), NIKKEI225, USATEC (indistinguishable: 0 % both ways), USDJPY (2026-10-07 audit), UKOIL and USOIL (32 XAUUSD H1 mothers' cross-market retest at the oos1 costs: tick 0 % vs none 72 % and 6 %).
- Why `none` wins: where the cost is not a tick multiple SQX lands on both neighbours with mean = the declared cost (gold oos1 entry .23 86 % / .22 14 %, declared .2286), so rounding adds a bias (gold −0.24 USD/trade in build) and the unrounded fill has none.
- Off-share (variants off by more than max(1 USD, 1 % of NetProfit)), tick → none: gold build 0-25 % → 0-1 %, oos1 28-77 % → 7-22 %; USA500 oos1 15.5 % → 0.6-1.2 %; DAX40 oos1 1-16 % → 0.2-3.7 %, build equal (8 vs 10 of 941: the «mixed» reading of 2026-10-13 was noise); NIKKEI oos1 0 % → 0.2-1.0 %.
- Gold oos1 leftover −0.043 USD/trade: the exit lands at .0587 on average against .0593 declared, drifting .0573-.0596 by year, both neighbour ticks always: sub-tick noise in SQX's bars that the stored bars lack. Not reproducible; accepted. So gold oos1 stays above the 18.5 gate (0.01).
- Ties: SQX rounds a half tickStep UP; `np.round` (half to even) put half the fills a tick off wherever spread+slippage is an exact half (GBPJPY oos1, AUDUSD build, AUDNZD oos1 — fixed 2026-10-15, OPEN #124 closed; AUDNZD none → tick). Re-measured after it, XAUUSD/USA500/DAX40 stay none (gold oos1 tick 65 % vs none 19 %). `measure(symbol, trades, segment)` takes the task's cost profile: `Sample type` IST says nothing.
- Not measured, hence refused: every asset with no batch on disk (other forex, metals other than gold); crossmarket marks those markets `cost_card_pending: price_rounding`. Family-retest exports label `Sample type` IST: pass `segment="oos1"` to `measure`, SQX ran them on oos1.
