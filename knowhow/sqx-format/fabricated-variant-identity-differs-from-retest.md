# A fabricated variant and its retested copy do not share `core.sqxfile.identity`
The `.sqx` the factory writes (`<batch>/sqx/S00V014.sqx`) hashes differently from the copy SQX hands
back after the retest, whose identity is what `export_retest` writes in `identity.csv`. So a batch
cannot be tied to its export by identity — only by the variant's file name inside ONE batch, and a
name like `S00V014` repeats in every batch. `pipeline.autopilot.traded.export` ties them by what is
left: the export was written after the batch's `ran.json` and holds as many strategies as its
manifest. `finalistas.json` carries the FABRICATED file's identity (the file step 26 is given, and
what `mt5.verify.autopilot.unfit` recomputes); the retest step 26 runs will carry another.

- `Test_USDJPY_donchianUpperCrossUp_H1`, step 24 pass 2 of 2026-09-30:
  `atrCalculator/<P>/pass2/sqx/S00V014.sqx` → `b415d7e50c66…`;
  `raw/<P>/WFC_Build|WFC_OOS1/2026-09-30/stopgrid/identity.csv`, `S00V014` → `1061d2aac782…`.
  Same for `S01V014` (`83c895d9…` vs `a9a16377…`) and `S02V014` (`2f79dda1…` vs `0db02097…`).
- The mother's own file is stable: `mothers/Strategy 10.9.72.sqx` = the harvest's `746c8da7…`.
- Not diffed: which element moves. A retest drops the `<!--variant_id-->` stamp
  (`sqx-format/writing-a-variant`) and identity strips only `makeExternal` and `autoGenerated`;
  a diff of the two `strategy_Portfolio.xml` would confirm that the stamp is all of it.
